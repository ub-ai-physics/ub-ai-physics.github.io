# Simple harmonic oscillator (example)

## Equation of motion

A mass \(m\) attached to a spring of constant \(k\) obeys Newton's second law:

\[
m\,\ddot{x} = -k\,x .
\]

With \(\omega = \sqrt{k/m}\), the general solution is

\[
x(t) = A\cos(\omega t + \varphi),
\]

where the amplitude \(A\) and the phase \(\varphi\) are fixed by the initial conditions.

## Energy

The total energy is conserved:

\[
E = \tfrac12 m\dot{x}^2 + \tfrac12 k x^2 = \tfrac12 k A^2 .
\]
